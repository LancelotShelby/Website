Study in Malaysia

Malaysia is a Southeast Asian country known for its diverse culture, modern cities, and beautiful landscapes. It is a popular destination for international students due to its high-quality education system and affordable living costs.

Application Requirements

Public Universities

The requirements for public universities in Malaysia may vary depending on the institution and program. Some programs may require additional entrance exams or interviews.

  • High School Diploma (original and notarized translation)
  • High School Transcript
  • Valid Passport
  • Language Proficiency Certificate (IELTS or equivalent)
  • STPM, A-Levels, or equivalent scores (if required by the university)
See Public Universities

Private Universities

Private universities often have more flexible admission criteria and shorter application windows. They may also offer scholarships based on academic merit.

  • High School Diploma (English or Malay, or a notarized translation in case the original is in another language)
  • High School Transcript
  • Valid Passport with a validity of at least 6 months
See Private Universities

Visa Information

Students from most countries will need to apply for a student visa (Student Pass) at the Malaysian embassy or consulate in their home country. A letter of acceptance from a recognized educational institution is required for the application.

Cost of Living

The cost of living in Malaysia is relatively low compared to many Western countries. Monthly expenses for a student can range from $300 to $800, depending on the city and lifestyle.
