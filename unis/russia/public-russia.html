Application Requirements

The requirements for public universities in Russia may vary depending on the institution and program. Some programs may require additional entrance exams or interviews.

Required Documents

  • High School Diploma (original and notarized translation)
  • High School Transcript
  • Valid Passport
  • Language Proficiency Certificate (TOEFL, IELTS, or Russian language proficiency test)
  • Unified State Exam (EGE) scores (if required by the university)
